Maybe a masterpiece in the future

Most of us are looking for the end of 2020. There was no better time to
release Cyberpunk 2077, even with all of its issues.

Let me tackle two of the problems mentioned by others, on PC:

1. Cannot configure the controls. There are ways to reach a working setup
by editing the configuration files

2. The driving is touchy. You'll get used to it, after killing a few people.
And it forces you to drive at reasonable speeds in a crowded city.
If you really find it difficult try a motorcycle, driving one seemed easier to me.
I personally find driving enjoyable, still haven't got bored of driving
through the city, from mission to mission.

Some other things I found that need improvement/fixing:

1. The map, it doesn't have basic options like: zoom, stop it from rotating,
it doesn't show the direction you are going (i.e. north, south, etc.)

2. Some perks have very few explanations. I took Hidden Dragon and I still
don't know how to actually use it. Gave up even on searching it on the net.

Respect for the people actually involved in the development of the game, for
starting on such a big and challenging project. Blame for the people who
didn't make it clear the game had problems, especially on the older
generation of consoles.
