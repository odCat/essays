The Northman

Lineages

The movie plays with the ideas of the three of life and lineages.
It suggests our ancestors were aware of the three of life. In truth
the three is an reoccurring occult symbol that seems to be an atavistic
representation of the DNA. Knowledge of which is often manifested of
religion experiences often induced by psychedelic substances of one
form or another.
The characters of the time portrayed seem to be locked in a competition
between lineages, also a very modern concept. But the characters seem
to be aware of this state of fact, as the various historical acts,
genocides, complete destruction and enslavement of people, and
beliefs, importance of women in tribal societies, attest.
It is funny to observe that in this competition women win even when they
lose.

Christianity breaking the pattern

One of the things civilization and Christianity did, was to break this
cycle of perpetual competition, often ended in brutal wars. Christianity
replaces the sacrifice of other like in war or ritual sacrifices with
the sacrifice of the self. And encourage to empathize with our fellow
humans. Latest idea is not unique to Christianity.

Christ - a corpse nailed to a three

One character makes a remark about Christians, that they worship a corpse
God, nailed to a three. A very apt description, in my opinion, since it
represents the best sacrifice to "life", which is the self.
